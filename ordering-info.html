Pickup is available in Montclair, California. All orders are available for pickup. Exact pickup details will be provided once your order is confirmed.

Ordering

Orders are accepted every day. We recommend placing your order at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day orders may be available depending on availability.

03

Wednesday Delivery

Delivery is available on Wednesdays. Delivery details will be confirmed with your order.

04

Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled before preparation begins. Once your order is being prepared or baked, it cannot be cancelled.

05

Allergy Notice

Please let Pam know about any food allergies when placing your order. Some products contain common allergens including wheat, milk, eggs, and tree nuts.
